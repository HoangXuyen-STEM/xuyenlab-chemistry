# P4.4 project-owner chemistry QA checklist

For each Topic 6/8 draft, record reviewer/date/time spent and check:

- Part I boundary and source provenance;
- terminology and explanatory text;
- every chemical formula/equation and numerical answer;
- tables, merged cells, units and headings;
- every figure against source, including meaningful alt/caption;
- examples, hints and worked solutions;
- mobile readability and A4 print output;
- every warning/blocking item in the generated report.

The owner may approve the draft to `in_review` after all blocking issues are resolved.
Do not set `published` during P4; production publication is a P7 decision after account,
authorization and signed-PDF UAT pass.
