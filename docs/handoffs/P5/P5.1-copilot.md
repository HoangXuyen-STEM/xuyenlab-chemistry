## Decisions and assumptions

- The content hash is defined exactly by the task packet: canonical MDX bytes only.
  Asset hashes are validated and recorded but do not enter content identity.
- PDF engine metadata may vary, so identity manifest determinism and object-key
  determinism are required; PDF byte equality is not claimed.
- P4's accepted lifecycle-aware validator remains the QA authority. P5.1 only checks
  that PDF inputs are `in_review`; it does not invent another QA-signature rule.
- Existing-object replacement is deliberately not exposed in the manual workflow.
  It remains blocked until backup/version identifiers and integration-owner approval
  exist.
