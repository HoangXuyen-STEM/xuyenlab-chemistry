## Decisions and assumptions

- The P3 mock always represents a student. Its teacher routes redirect to
  `/khong-co-quyen`; teacher components exist for the P3.1 server facade to expose
  after `requireTeacher` is available. This is presentation behavior only, never an
  authorization boundary (ADR-0003).
- PDF deliberately displays a truthful staging-unavailable state after its typed
  facade call. P3.3 owns the authenticated signed-URL endpoint.
- The fixture uses no canonical content file, no `published` status and no chemistry
  claim requiring owner approval (content contract).
